# Endpoint inventory re-measurement — audit-v10 (T4.1)

The v9 baseline recorded **144 mappings / 26 controllers**
(`.specify/specs/audit-v9-full/endpoint-map.md`, generated by `sweep/v8/endpoint_inventory.py`).

A naive grep for the five verb annotations returns **131 occurrences in 26 files**, which looks
like a 13-route regression. **It is not.** The grep is the wrong instrument, and the arithmetic
reconciles exactly.

## Why the two numbers differ

14 annotations carry **two path literals each** (`{"/api/v1/...", "/api/v1/video-..."}` — the
speaking/video alias pairs). One annotation becomes two routes.

| File | Multi-path annotations |
|---|---|
| `controller/speaking/SpeakingPromptController.java` | 7 |
| `controller/speaking/SpeakingSubmissionController.java` | 7 |

## Reconciliation by HTTP verb

| Verb | Annotations | Multi-path | Routes from multi | Total routes | v9 baseline |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GET | 56 | 6 | 12 | 56 − 6 + 12 = **62** | 61 |
| POST | 49 | 5 | 10 | 49 − 5 + 10 = **54** | 54 |
| PUT | 15 | 1 | 2 | 15 − 1 + 2 = **16** | 16 |
| DELETE | 9 | 1 | 2 | 9 − 1 + 2 = **10** | 10 |
| PATCH | 2 | 1 | 2 | 2 − 1 + 2 = **3** | 3 |
| **Total** | **131** | **14** | **28** | **145** | **144** |

145 − 144 = **1 duplicate**, which the inventory script removes (it dedupes on
`(controller, httpMethod, path)`). GET is the verb carrying that duplicate, which is why the GET
row is the only one that overshoots by exactly 1.

**Conclusion: the endpoint surface is unchanged at 144 routes / 26 controllers.** No route was
added or lost by the audit-v10 code changes. The 131 figure is an artefact of counting
annotations instead of routes.

## Method note for the next audit

Do not count endpoint annotations with a plain grep. Either run
`sweep/v8/endpoint_inventory.py`, or count annotations **plus** the extra path literals inside
`{...}` annotation arguments. Recorded here because the wrong method produced a 13-route
phantom regression that cost real time to disprove.
