## SECTION E — Gaps that need a real browser (not verifiable from code)
**E1 Font loading (priority).** Whether Vietnamese diacritics actually render from the subset Google returns (no `&subset=` pin), the FOUT/FOIT window at throttled network with `display=swap`, whether the not-defeated `App.vue` injected stylesheet 404s in production and visibly re-flows type, and whether `font-weight:800` ever renders anywhere (markup uses only 700/900).
**E2 Mobile pop shadow (priority).** Computed `box-shadow` at 375px for `.shadow-pop*`, `hover:shadow-pop-*` (17), `active:shadow-pop-*` (3) and `shadow-[…]` (12) — the `@media` patch (`design-system.css:594-615`) may lose to Tailwind's own source order for hover/active variants; only DevTools can prove the 2px reduction actually applies (B-02).
**E3 Contrast (priority).** All ratios in A2b/B-06/B-07/B-08 are hex math; `opacity:.15` blobs, `/10`–`/35` alpha chips, `grayscale` card images and `text-shadow`/`drop-shadow` change effective contrast. Needs a per-view axe/DevTools contrast audit — specifically `Home.vue:30`, `Profile.vue:20,43`, `AdminDashboard.vue:19,20`, `LessonPreview.vue:31,59`, `TypingGame.vue:51,75`, plus the 20 `text-secondary` alert sites.
**E4 Reduced-motion behavior (priority).** With `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` on, confirm no card/button visibly jumps (`transition-duration:0.01ms` still applies the transform), confirm `scroll-behavior:smooth` still scrolls instantly (not gated, B-01), and confirm the navbar mobile panel / modal pop-in do not animate.
